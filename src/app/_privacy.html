Privacy Policy – Mintflare.fun

At Mintflare.fun, we value your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we handle your data when you use our platform.

1. Data Collection

We do not collect personal or identifiable information such as names, email addresses, or phone numbers. However, we use analytics tools to monitor site performance and user behavior in an anonymous and aggregated manner.

2. Wallet Connection

We use third-party wallet providers (e.g., Phantom, Solflare) to enable blockchain interactions. While your wallet address is visible during your session, it is never stored or logged by Mintflare.fun.

3. Analytics

We use Plausible Analytics, a privacy-friendly and cookie-less analytics platform. It helps us understand how users interact with our website without collecting personally identifiable information or setting any cookies.

4. External Services

Our site may contain links to third-party services (such as wallet providers). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these external sites.

5. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
